## 8. Calls, texts and consent (SMS / TCPA)

**You are the sender of record.** Calls are answered and texts are sent in your business's name, on your behalf. You are responsible for having any consent the law requires. To help you, the service collects and logs consent in the ways described below, and you agree not to change these settings. We do not give legal advice and do not promise that these steps are enough for every caller or every state.

1. **Inbound only.** The AI answers calls that callers make to you. It does not place outbound calls with an artificial or AI voice.
2. **Notice in the greeting.** Every AI-answered call starts with a notice that the caller is speaking with an AI assistant, that the call may be recorded and transcribed by us and our service providers, and that the business may text the caller at that number about the call. You cannot remove or shorten this notice.
3. **Texts we send, and the consent basis for each.**  
   (a) *Missed-call text:* at most one text per missed call (and no more than one per number in 24 hours), sent soon after the caller's own call, with fixed wording that names your business and says "Reply STOP to opt out." You can change only your business name in it. Wording: "Hi, this is [Business Name]. Sorry we missed your call! What can we help with? Reply STOP to opt out."  
   (b) *Booking confirmation and one reminder:* sent only if the caller said yes when the assistant asked, during the call or the text conversation, whether it may text a confirmation and a reminder. The answer is logged with the time and the call record. If the caller says no, the booking still stands and the assistant gives the details out loud instead.  
   (c) *Replies:* the AI texts back only in a conversation the caller started or continued by texting.  
   No marketing, promotional, review-request or sales texts are sent through the service, and you agree not to ask for them or add such content to any template.
4. **Opt-out.** STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, any message that clearly asks not to be texted, and a spoken request on a call are honored right away for all automated messages to that number, until the person texts START. One confirmation text may be sent. If a caller asks you directly (in person, by email or on your own phone) not to be texted, you must tell us, and we will add the number to the opt-out list within one business day.
5. **Time window.** A booking confirmation is sent right after the call, because it answers the caller's yes on that call. A missed-call text is sent right after the call, but not between 9:00 p.m. and 8:00 a.m.; if it has to wait, it is sent only once, in the window below, and only if the caller has not called back. Reminders and any other message that is not a direct reply to the caller are sent only between 9:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. (noon to 8:00 p.m. on Sundays). Each window is checked in both your time zone and the caller's likely time zone (from the area code).
6. **Landlines.** The system checks the number type and does not text landlines.
7. **Records.** We keep a log of the consent basis, time and number for each text, and of each opt-out, for five years, and we will give you a copy on request to answer a complaint or claim.
8. **SMS registration (A2P 10DLC).** We register your business with US carriers as your technology provider, and we describe the opt-in to carriers truthfully: callers hear the texting notice in the greeting, the assistant asks before booking texts, and STOP and HELP are supported. You confirm that the business details you give us for registration are true. Registration can be rejected or delayed by carriers; this is outside our control.

**AI and recording notice.** Every AI-answered call begins with a spoken notice that the caller is speaking with an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded and transcribed by us and our service providers. The notice plays before the assistant processes anything the caller says, and you cannot remove or shorten it. If a caller asks whether they are talking to a person, the AI answers honestly. Call audio is streamed to our service providers to convert speech to text and text to speech and is not stored by us. Transcripts are kept for 90 days and then deleted. If a caller objects to recording or transcription, the assistant stops, does not keep a transcript of the conversation, and alerts you to call the person back. We process call content only to provide the service to you and do not use it to train AI models; our service providers process it under their own terms, which limit how they may use it. The laws of some states require all parties to a call to consent to its recording or interception. The notice is designed so that a caller who continues the call after hearing it consents, which is the approach courts and statutes in those states generally accept. You will not instruct callers or use the service in a way that conflicts with the notice, and you will tell us if you need a different notice for a specific state or industry.

You represent that: (a) you have the right to receive and respond to calls and texts at the numbers you forward to us; (b) you will not ask us to text anyone who has not called or texted you, or to send marketing texts; (c) you will promptly forward to us any opt-out request you receive outside the service; and (d) you will not use the service in a way that breaks the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, state telemarketing or call-recording laws, or carrier rules. You will indemnify us for claims caused by your breach of this section, including content you add and contacts you ask us to make. We do not claim that the service makes your business compliant with any law.

**Compliance features are not legal advice.** The default wording, opt-out handling and other compliance features we build are tools to help you comply; they are not legal advice, and we are not your compliance advisor or fiduciary. You are responsible for insuring your own business, including against claims by your callers.
