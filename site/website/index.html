FAQ

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

$1,900 setup and $129 a month. The deposit is $950; the rest is due before launch. The monthly fee covers keeping the site online and secure, checking that it's up, and 1 small edit per month.

Who writes the text?

We draft it with AI from your questionnaire answers, then you approve every page. Nothing goes live without your OK.

Do I own the domain?

Yes. If you already have a domain, we connect it. If not, you register one in your own name and we show you how.

Can I bring my own photos?

Yes, and we recommend it. Real photos of your team and trucks work better than stock. We use stock photos only where you have none.

Will it show up on Google?

We set up the basics so Google can read your site: page titles and descriptions, your business details in Google's format, a list of your pages, Google's free site tools, and a link to your Google Business Profile. Where you rank depends on many things, so we can't promise a spot.

What if I need more pages or Spanish?

Extra pages are $250 each. A full Spanish version is $900 + $20/mo. Choosing an extra adds a short written quote step.

Do I have to get on a call with you?

No. There's no quote step: choose the package on this page, fill in the 10-minute questionnaire, and we email your deposit link within 1 business day. If you'd rather talk first, you can book an optional 15-minute video call.

Can I cancel?

The monthly plan cancels with 30 days' notice. Changed your mind? Ask within 7 days of paying and we refund your full deposit, as long as you haven't finished the setup checklist.
