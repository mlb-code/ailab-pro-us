FAQ

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

$1,900 setup and $129 a month. The deposit is $950; the rest is due before launch. The monthly fee covers hosting, SSL, an uptime check and 1 small content edit per month.

Who writes the text?

We draft it with AI from your questionnaire answers, then you approve every page. Nothing goes live without your OK.

Do I own the domain?

Yes. If you already have a domain, we connect it. If not, you register one in your own name and we show you how.

Can I bring my own photos?

Yes, and we recommend it. Real photos of your team and trucks work better than stock. We use stock photos only where you have none.

Will it show up on Google?

We set up the basics: titles and descriptions, LocalBusiness schema, a sitemap, Google Search Console and a link to your Google Business Profile. Rankings depend on many things; we don't promise positions.

What if I need more pages or Spanish?

Extra pages are $250 each. A full Spanish version is $900 + $20/mo. Choosing an add-on adds a short written quote step.

Do I have to get on a call with you?

No. There's no quote step: choose the package on this page, pay the deposit, and fill in the content questionnaire. If you'd rather talk first, you can book an optional 15-minute video call.

Can I cancel?

The monthly plan cancels with 30 days notice. The setup deposit is fully refunded on request within 7 days of payment, as long as the access checklist has not been completed.
