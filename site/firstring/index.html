What does it cost, exactly?

One monthly price, no setup fee. Starter is $149 a month with 200 voice minutes, Pro is $249 with 500, and Business is $399 with 1,000. Pro and Business also answer in Spanish and include a chat box on your website (set up for you on request). Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial (up to 100 minutes): we need a card to start, and the first charge comes when the trial ends. Never billed per minute: alerts at 80% and 100% of your minutes, a one-click upgrade, and never an extra charge without your yes.

Do I keep my business number?

Yes. When you don't answer or the line is busy, your phone sends the call to the new number we set up (this is called call forwarding). Calls you pick up never reach it. Our setup checklist shows how for Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, Comcast Business, RingCentral and Google Voice.

Can I cancel? What if I'm not happy?

Yes, cancel anytime in your online account. Cancel during the free trial and you pay nothing. After that, your plan stops at the end of the month you paid for. 30-day money-back guarantee: not happy? Ask within 30 days of your first payment and we refund that first monthly payment in full. The details are in our Refund Policy.
