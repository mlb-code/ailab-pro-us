Refund Policy

Version 2.2 · Effective date: November 27, 2026 · Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Previous version 2.1 effective November 25, 2026

Plain-English summary. Try FirstRing free for 14 days; cancel during the trial and you pay nothing. Not happy after you start paying? Ask within 30 days of your first payment and we refund that first month in full. After that you can cancel anytime: your plan stops at the end of the month you paid for, and we do not refund partial months.

1. FirstRing monthly plans

  • Free trial. Every new business starts with a free trial of 14 days (up to 100 voice minutes). We need a payment card to start it, but nothing is charged during the trial. Cancel in your online account before the trial ends and you are never charged; the trial then ends right away and FirstRing switches to message mode.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with your FirstRing plan, ask us in writing (email or your online account) within 30 days after your first paid charge, and we will refund that first monthly payment in full and end the plan. This applies once per business, to the first paid month only. After that, monthly fees are not refunded for partial months or for months already started, unless a refund is legally required.
  • After that. You can cancel anytime in your online account. The plan stops at the end of the month you have already paid for, and you are not charged again. We confirm the cancellation by email. We do not refund partial months or months already started.
  • Plan changes. If you upgrade, the prorated charge for the rest of the current month is not refunded when you later downgrade or cancel. A downgrade takes effect on your next billing date.
  • Charges you did not approve. We never charge per minute or add extras without your yes. If you see a charge you did not expect, tell us and we will refund any charge we made in error.

2. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@ailabpro.co or write to us from your online account, with your business name. We reply within one business day (Sunday–Thursday, Israel time). Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Paddle.com, our reseller and the merchant of record for monthly plans. Card refunds usually show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. You can also request a refund directly from Paddle at paddle.net. Paddle may issue refunds under its own Buyer Terms, and nothing in this policy limits that.

3. Business websites and other quoted work

  • Setup deposit: fully refunded on request within 7 days of payment, as long as you have not completed the setup (access) checklist. For the website on its own, this is the only setup refund, because content work starts right away.
  • FirstRing Quotes signed before November 25, 2026 (setup fee plus monthly fee): the 30-day satisfaction guarantee in your Quote and in section 5 of the Service Agreement still applies.
  • Retainer plans (Care, Growth, Studio): if you cancel within 7 days of your first retainer payment, we refund 75% of that first payment.
  • Monthly website plans: no refunds for partial months or months already used.
  • Payments made through Upwork are refunded through Upwork.

4. Your other rights

This policy does not limit any right to a refund that applicable rules give you and that cannot be waived. The Terms of Service and the Service Agreement say the same as this page; if they ever differ, the one more favorable to you applies to refunds.
