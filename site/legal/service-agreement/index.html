8. Calls, texts and consent (SMS / TCPA)

The Missed-Call AI Receptionist works like this, and you agree to use it this way:

  1. Inbound only. The AI answers calls that callers make to you. It does not place outbound calls with an artificial or AI voice.
  2. Text-back only after the caller's own call. When a caller calls you and the call is missed or ends, the system may send that caller a text about their request. Texts are informational (request follow-up, booking confirmation, booking reminder). No marketing or promotional texts are sent through the service.
  3. Identification and opt-out. The first text in each conversation names your business and says "Reply STOP to opt out." Default wording: "Hi, this is [Business Name]. Sorry we missed your call. How can we help? Reply STOP to opt out."
  4. STOP is honored right away and permanently for that line, across all automated messages (text-back, confirmations, reminders and AI replies), until the person replies START.
  5. Quiet hours. Messages that are not a direct reply to the caller are not sent between 9:00 p.m. and 8:00 a.m. in your time zone.
  6. Landlines. The system checks the number type and does not text landlines.
  7. AI disclosure. The greeting says the caller is speaking with an automated assistant. If a caller asks whether they are talking to a person, the AI answers honestly.
  8. Transcription notice. The greeting says "This call may be transcribed." We do not record call audio. Some states require all parties to consent to recording or interception of calls. You are responsible for deciding whether this notice is enough for your callers' states, and for any added notice or consent your attorney advises.
  9. SMS registration (A2P 10DLC). We register your business with US carriers as your technology provider. You confirm that the business details you give us for registration are true. Registration can be rejected or delayed by carriers; this is outside our control.

You represent that: (a) you have the right to receive and respond to calls and texts at the numbers you forward to us; (b) you will not ask us to send texts to anyone who has not called you, or to send marketing texts; and (c) you will not use the service in a way that breaks the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, state telemarketing or call-recording laws, or carrier rules. We do not claim that the service makes your business compliant with any law.
