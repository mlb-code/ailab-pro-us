Plain-English summary. We collect what we need to quote, build and run your service. For your callers, we keep call transcripts and text messages for 90 days, then delete them. We do not record call audio. We do not sell personal information. We do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data for marketing.

1. Who we are

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME], doing business as [BRAND NAME], [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. Privacy contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

2. Two kinds of information

  • Our clients' information (business owners and their staff who use our website, questionnaire and portal). For this information, we decide how it is used.
  • Callers' information (people who call or text one of our client businesses through the AI line). We process this information on behalf of that business, as its service provider, only to provide the service. The business decides how it is used. If you are a caller, you can also contact the business directly.

3. What we collect

3.1 From clients

  • Contact and account data: name, business name, email, phone, time zone, language preference.
  • Questionnaire answers: services, hours, service area, tools you use, FAQ content.
  • Quote and contract data: accepted Quote, the name you typed to accept, date, IP address, browser type, and the document version you agreed to.
  • Billing data: invoices and payment status. Card or account details are handled by the payment provider (for example PayPal or Upwork); we do not see full card numbers.
  • Connection data: access tokens for tools you connect (for example Google Calendar or Calendly). We store them encrypted.
  • Support data: requests and messages you send us.

3.2 From callers (on behalf of the business)

  • Phone number, and what the caller says or types: name, service address or ZIP, job type, urgency, preferred time.
  • Call transcripts (speech converted to text) and text message content. We do not store call audio. One exception: if our system is down, the phone carrier plays a voicemail greeting and stores the caller's voicemail, with notice in the greeting.
  • Booking details created in the business's calendar.
  • Opt-out status (STOP / START).
  • Call and message metadata: time, duration, delivery status, line type (mobile or landline).

3.3 From website visitors

  • Usage data from analytics tools (for example Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity): pages viewed, device, approximate location, clicks and scrolls. These tools use cookies or similar technology.
  • Text you type into the website demo. The demo is fictional; do not type personal information.

4. How we use information

  • To write your Quote, build and run your service, and support you.
  • To answer calls and texts for the business, book appointments, and send the business lead alerts and summaries.
  • To bill you, keep records, and meet legal and tax duties.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To improve our service using aggregated or de-identified data.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use callers' information for our own marketing.

Mobile numbers and SMS consent. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver the messages (such as our SMS carrier).

5. AI processing

We use Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, PBC, through Anthropic's commercial API, to understand callers, write replies and draft Quotes. Text from calls and messages is sent to the API to produce each answer. We do not opt in to any program that allows Anthropic to use this data to train its models. 8. Security

We use encryption in transit, encrypted storage of access tokens, access controls per client, signed webhooks, and the minimum access we need. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

9. International transfers

We are based in Israel, and our main systems are in the United States. Your information may be accessed from Israel to provide support and maintenance.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California under the CCPA/CPRA, and other US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we have about you and how we use it;
  • get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • opt out of sale or sharing (we do not sell or share for advertising);
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will confirm your identity before acting, and answer within the time the law requires (usually 45 days). An authorized agent may make a request for you with your written permission. If you are a caller of one of our client businesses, we will pass your request to that business and help it respond. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out request.

11. Health and other sensitive information

Our services are not designed to collect health, financial-account or government-ID information. The AI asks only for name, phone number and a general reason for the call. We are not a HIPAA business associate and do not sign business associate agreements. Clients must not configure our services to collect protected health information.

12. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.
