Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Plain-English summary. We collect what we need to quote, build and run your service. For your callers, we keep call transcripts and text messages for 90 days, then delete them. We do not record call audio. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data for marketing.

1. Who we are

Lavi Entrepreneurship & Management (Meir Lavi), doing business as AI Lab Pro ("we", "us"), 29 Yigal Alon St., Herzliya, Israel. Privacy contact: support@ailabpro.co.

This Policy covers our website, the online questionnaire, the client portal and the services we run for our clients. It is part of our Terms of Service.

2. Two kinds of information

  • Our clients' information (business owners and their staff who use our website, questionnaire and portal). For this information, we decide how it is used.
  • Callers' information (people who call or text one of our client businesses through the AI line). We process this information on behalf of that business, as its service provider, only to provide the service. The business decides how it is used and is responsible for its own privacy notice to its customers. If you are a caller, you can also contact the business directly.

3. What we collect

3.1 From clients

  • Contact and account data: name, business name, email, phone, time zone, language preference.
  • Questionnaire answers: services, hours, service area, tools you use, FAQ content.
  • Quote and contract data: accepted Quote, the name you typed to accept, date, IP address, browser type, and the document version you agreed to.
  • Billing data: invoices and payment status. Card or account details are handled by the payment provider (for example PayPal, Payoneer or Upwork); we do not see full card numbers.
  • Connection data: access tokens for tools you connect (for example Google Calendar or Calendly). We store them encrypted.
  • Support data: requests and messages you send us.

3.2 From callers (on behalf of the business)

  • Phone number, and what the caller says or types: name, service address or ZIP, job type, urgency, preferred time.
  • Call transcripts (speech converted to text) and text message content. We do not store call audio. One exception: if our system is down, the phone carrier plays a voicemail greeting and stores the caller's voicemail, with notice in the greeting.
  • Booking details created in the business's calendar.
  • Opt-out status (STOP / START).
  • Call and message metadata: time, duration, delivery status, line type (mobile or landline).

3.3 From website visitors

  • Usage data from analytics tools (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity): pages viewed, device, approximate location, clicks and scrolls. These tools use cookies or similar technology. You can block cookies in your browser, use Google's opt-out add-on for Google Analytics, or send a Global Privacy Control signal.
  • Text you type into the website demo. The demo is fictional; do not type personal information.

4. How we use information

  • To write your Quote, build and run your service, and support you.
  • To answer calls and texts for the business, book appointments, and send the business lead alerts and summaries.
  • To bill you, keep records, and meet legal and tax duties.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To improve our service using aggregated or de-identified data.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising. We do not use callers' information for our own marketing.

Mobile numbers and SMS consent. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver the messages (such as our SMS carrier).

5. AI processing

We use Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, PBC, through Anthropic's commercial API, to understand callers, write replies and draft Quotes. Text from calls and messages is sent to the API to produce each answer. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default, and we have not opted in to any program that would allow it. The AI does not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on anyone; your business reviews and controls what the AI does. We are not affiliated with Anthropic.
