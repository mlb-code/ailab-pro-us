What happens to a missed call

The caller doesn't wait. Your receptionist doesn't either.

  1. 01

    It answers

    Calls you don't pick up go to your AI receptionist's number. It answers with your business name and asks what's going on. (Texting callers who hang up is coming soon, after US carrier registration.)

  2. 02

    It asks the right questions

    Name, callback number, address or ZIP, job type, urgency. Emergencies like a gas smell get a 911 instruction first and an urgent alert to you.

  3. 03

    It books, you get an email

    It books only open times on your calendar, in the areas and hours you serve. You get each new lead by email, plus a daily summary (text alerts too, once business texting is live).

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I keep my business number?

Yes. When you don't answer or the line is busy, your phone sends the call to the new number we set up (this is called call forwarding). Calls you pick up never reach it. Our setup checklist shows how for Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, Comcast Business, RingCentral and Google Voice.

What does it cost, exactly?

$3,500 setup and $300 a month. You pay $1,750 to start and the rest before it goes live (the day it starts answering real customers). The monthly fee starts the day it goes live. 400 minutes included every month (1,000 texts included too, once business texting is live). We let you know when you've used 80%. No surprise charges: we add more only if you say yes.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as your virtual assistant and never claims to be a person. The greeting says the call may be recorded and transcribed by us and our service providers, and that we may text the caller about it. Texting is coming soon, after US carrier registration; once it is live, it asks the caller's permission before it texts a booking confirmation or reminder, and saves their answer. If a caller doesn't want to be recorded, it stops and keeps no written copy. We keep written copies of calls, never the audio.

Can I hear it before I buy?

Yes. Call +1 832-307-0813. It reaches our demo business, Copperleaf Air & Plumbing in Houston, and the same AI receptionist answers. It's a US number, so regular call charges apply, and the call may be recorded and written down as text. You can also try the text demo on this page.

What about emergencies?

You choose the emergency keywords (gas smell, carbon monoxide, flooding, no heat with a baby at home). If there may be danger to life, the caller is told to hang up and call 911 first. Otherwise you get an URGENT email right away (and a text, once business texting is live), and it can ring your cell.

Why not the AI assistant built into Jobber or Housecall Pro?

Built-in assistants are a good fit if you only need booking inside that app. Ours answers the phone itself, passes the caller to a person when needed, and a real person keeps it running. It works with Google Calendar, Calendly and most apps through Zapier. Some systems (for example Outlook or ServiceTitan) need a custom quote.

Does it work with Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan?

Booking into Google Calendar or Calendly is included. So is 1 Zapier link (Zapier is an app that connects apps) to send leads to any other tool. A direct link to Jobber, Housecall Pro, GoHighLevel or HubSpot costs $1,200 + $50/mo. For ServiceTitan, we quote separately.

Do I have to get on a call with you?

No. You fill in a questionnaire, get a written quote within 1 business day, and finish a setup checklist with short videos. If you want a 15-minute video call anyway, you can book one. It's optional.

Can I cancel? What if I'm not happy?

30-day satisfaction guarantee: not happy within 30 days of go-live (the day it starts answering real customers)? We refund the setup fee for your AI assistant and turn it off. On the bundle, we refund only the AI assistant's part of the setup fee, and you keep the website. Monthly fees you already used are not refunded. Changed your mind? Ask within 7 days of paying and we refund your full deposit, as long as you haven't finished the setup checklist. Monthly service cancels with 30 days' notice.
