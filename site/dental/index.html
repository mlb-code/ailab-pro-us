FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it handle patient health information?

No. It collects only a name, a phone number, new or existing patient, and a general reason such as "cleaning" or "tooth pain". It doesn't give medical advice. We don't sign a HIPAA BAA in this version, which is one reason this is early access.

Does it connect to Dentrix or Open Dental?

Not as a standard feature. Booking goes into Google Calendar or Calendly, and your team copies it into your practice system. A direct connection gets a custom quote.

Do I keep my business number?

Yes. You set call forwarding on no-answer or busy to the AI line we set up. Calls you pick up never reach it. Our checklist shows how for Verizon, AT&T, T-Mobile, Comcast Business, RingCentral and Google Voice.

What does it cost, exactly?

$3,500 setup and $300 a month. You pay a $1,750 deposit to start and the rest before go-live. The monthly fee starts on your go-live date. 400 minutes + 1,000 texts included every month. We alert you at 80%. No surprise charges: extra usage is added only if you approve it.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as your virtual assistant and never claims to be a person. The greeting says the call may be transcribed. We keep transcripts, not audio.

Do I have to get on a call with you?

No. You fill in a questionnaire, get a written quote within 1 business day (business days are Sunday–Thursday, Israel time), and finish a setup checklist with short videos. If you want a 15-minute video call anyway, you can book one. It's optional.

When will it be available?

After a legal review for dental practices. Early-access practices hear from us first, by email.

Can I cancel? What if I'm not happy?

30-day satisfaction guarantee: if you're not satisfied within 30 days after go-live, we refund the setup fee and switch the line off. Monthly fees for the period used are not refunded. The setup deposit is fully refunded on request within 7 days of payment, as long as the access checklist has not been completed. Monthly service cancels with 30 days notice.
